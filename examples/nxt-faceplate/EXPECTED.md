# Expected behaviour

- On start: mono_lcd widget (100×64) renders, 3 buttons visible, display shows "NXT 2.0\nTouch: --\nLight: --".
- Press ▶: mode advances to 1 (motor status).
- Press ▶ again: mode 2 (battery/BT/USB info).
- Press ◀: back to mode 1.
- Press ●: display shows "mode 1 selected".
- Press ▶ from mode 2: wraps to mode 0.
