# Salt makes water conduct

**Teaches:** conductivity, electrolyte, decomposition voltage

## What to do

Click **Sim**: the wires are in demineralised water (slide switch slide_switch_5 left). Then slide it right: the same water with a pinch of salt.

## What you should see

- Demineralised water: the LED stays dark.
- Salt water: the LED lights.

## Why it works

Water conducts only through the ions dissolved in it. Demineralised water has almost none; salt splits into sodium and chloride ions as it dissolves, and they carry the current. Where the current enters and leaves the water, it splits water into gas, which needs about 1.7 V of its own.
