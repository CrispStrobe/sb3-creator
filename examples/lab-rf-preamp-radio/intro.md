# Radio with RF stage and transistor detector

**Teaches:** RF amplifier, detector, emitter follower

## What to do

Click **Sim** and listen to the earphone (earphone_20): the station on 612 kHz. Slide DETECTOR (slide_switch_15) right to switch from the diode to the transistor detector. Turn the tuning dial (tuning_cap_7) to find the other station.

## What you should see

- Diode detector: the 400 Hz station, quietly.
- Transistor detector: the same station, much louder.
- Dial at about 0.27: a 600 Hz station.

## Why it works

The first transistor amplifies the radio wave itself before anything else happens. A detector then cuts away one half of the wave so that the programme is left. A diode does just that; a transistor wired as an emitter follower does it with its base-emitter junction and also amplifies the current, so the earphone gets much more.
