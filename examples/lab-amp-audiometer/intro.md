# Hearing-range tester

How high can you hear? Turn the knob and the tone climbs from a comfortable whistle to a hiss at the edge of hearing. Mark where it disappears for you — and compare with someone older or younger.

**Teaches:** RC oscillator, pitch control, hearing limit

## What to do

Click **Sim** and turn the knob slowly from 0 to 1.

## What you should see

- The tone rises from about 770 Hz to about 12.8 kHz.
- Near the top, many people stop hearing it.

## Why it works

The knob changes the resistance through which the timing capacitor charges. Less resistance means faster charging and more flips per second: a higher note.
