# Heat dims an LED, and sharpens a sensor

Two identical light links sit side by side. A heater warms the LED of the first and the phototransistor of the second. One meter falls, the other rises.

**Teaches:** temperature dependence of LEDs and phototransistors

## What to do

Click **Sim** and compare the two meters. Switch the heater on and watch for a few seconds. Then switch it off again.

## What you should see

- Heater off: both meters about 1.5 V.
- Heater on: meter 1 falls to about 0.84 V, meter 2 rises to about 1.83 V.
- Heater off: both drift back.

## Why it works

In a warm LED more of the electrons lose their energy as heat instead of light, so the same current gives less light. In a warm phototransistor the transistor's current gain rises, so the same light gives more current. Both effects matter whenever light is used to measure something.
