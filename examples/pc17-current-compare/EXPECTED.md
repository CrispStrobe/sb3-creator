# pc17-current-compare — three LEDs, three resistors

## Circuit
Three parallel paths with 220Ω, 470Ω, and 1kΩ resistors. Shows how
resistance controls brightness.

## Expected
- 220Ω: I = 13.6 mA, brightness ≈ 0.6522 (brightest)
- 470Ω: I = 6.4 mA, brightness ≈ 0.3125
- 1kΩ:  I = 3.0 mA, brightness ≈ 0.1485 (dimmest)
