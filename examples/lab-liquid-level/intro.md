# A light beam that knows when the glass is full

A light beam crosses a round glass to a phototransistor. As soon as the liquid reaches the beam, it bends the light away, the phototransistor goes dark, and a blue LED says "full".

**Teaches:** refraction, a sensor that never touches the liquid

## What to do

Click **Sim** and slowly turn the knob to fill the glass.

## What you should see

- Empty or below the beam: the blue LED stays off.
- Once the level passes the beam: the blue LED lights.

## Why it works

Light passes straight through the thin glass walls of an empty glass. Liquid in a round glass acts like a lens: it bends the beam off to the side and spreads it out, so hardly any of it reaches the phototransistor. With less light, the phototransistor stops holding the transistor's base low, and the transistor switches the LED on.
