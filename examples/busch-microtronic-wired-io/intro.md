## Busch Microtronic 2090

Press **Run** on the Microtronic in Instruments. Toggle **IN1**, **IN2** or **IN3** under Switches and watch the corresponding LED. **OUT4** blinks because the separate **CLOCK** terminal is wired to **IN4**.

The program reads the four inputs, writes that value to the four outputs, and repeats. Press **Halt** to hold the outputs or **Step** to execute one instruction.
