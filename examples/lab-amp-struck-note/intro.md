# Struck note that dies away

Strike a key and the note rings out and fades, like a piano string — because the tone generator lives off a capacitor that empties while it plays.

**Teaches:** capacitor as energy store, envelope, oscillator supply

## What to do

Click **Sim**. Tap the button (the key) briefly, then listen. Tap it again.

## What you should see

- Each tap: a loud note that fades within a fraction of a second.
- The pitch stays the same while it fades.

## Why it works

While the key is down the capacitor fills from the battery. Let go and the amplifier draws its current from the capacitor alone; its voltage sinks, and the amplifier's output can swing less and less until it stops working. The switching thresholds are fixed fractions of that same voltage, so the note keeps its pitch.
