## What you see
Two 555 blinkers, red and green, running at almost the same speed. A yellow LED lights only while both are on at the same moment, and a speaker hears both mixed together.

## Try this
1. Click **Sim** and watch red and green. They start together, drift apart and come back together.
2. Watch the yellow LED: its flashes swell and fade, once every two seconds.
3. Turn **tune B** slowly up. The swelling gets slower — you are tuning B towards A.
4. Find the point where the yellow LED's flashes stay the same: zero beat, both run at the same speed.

## What is going on
Two oscillations with almost the same frequency keep slipping against each other. Every time the faster one has gained exactly one whole cycle, they are back in step. How often that happens is simply the difference of the two frequencies: 5.5 minus 5 gives one meeting every two seconds. With sound you hear this as a slow wobble in loudness — musicians use it to tune two strings, and radios use the same difference trick to move a station to a fixed frequency.
