# A tone bridge: the earphone falls silent at balance

Before electronic meters, bridges were balanced by ear: feed them a tone, listen with an earphone across the middle, and turn the dial until the tone vanishes. Here the bridge holds a silicon PTC sensor, and the tone tells you when its temperature has moved.

**Teaches:** AC bridges, null detection, silicon PTC sensors

## What to do

Click **Sim** and listen to the earphone. Use the **Temperature** slider of the sensor in the instruments column, then try the balance potentiometer.

## What you should see

- At 25 °C the earphone is silent.
- Warmer or colder, the tone returns, and it grows the further you go from 25 °C.

## Why it works

Both sides of the bridge divide the same tone. When their ratios match, the two middle points move exactly together and no current flows through the earphone. When the sensor's resistance changes, one side moves more than the other and the difference is heard. Your ear is very sensitive to a faint tone, which made this a precise way to measure resistance.
