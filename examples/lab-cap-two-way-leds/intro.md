# Capacitor current both ways

**Teaches:** capacitor, charge and discharge, current direction

## What to do

Click **Sim**: the slide switch (slide_switch_3) is at CHARGE. Watch the red LED. Then slide it to DISCHARGE and watch the green one. Go back and forth, and listen to the earphone.

## What you should see

- At CHARGE the red LED flashes and fades within about half a second.
- At DISCHARGE the green LED does the same.
- The earphone clicks at every throw.

## Why it works

Charging pushes current into the capacitor one way, discharging takes it out the other way. The two LEDs point in opposite directions, so each one shows one direction. The current is biggest right after switching and dies away as the capacitor fills or empties.
