## Table score

Import the monitor ROM, run until ready, and pause the CPU. Load the cells, resume without resetting, and enter `000 PC RUN`.

Connect a digital tone decoder to P2. Notes use codes 3, 5, and 8; zero is silence. Run to hear or observe six slots, each held for 120 units. The final zero switches the sound off.
