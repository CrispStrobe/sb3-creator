# Voice canceller: left minus right

On many records the singer is mixed into the middle: exactly the same in the left and the right channel. Subtract right from left and the voice disappears, while instruments placed to one side stay.

**Teaches:** difference amplifier, common-mode rejection, stereo

## What to do

Click **Sim** and open the oscilloscope. Slide **CANCEL** to "left − right" and back. To hear one source alone, set the other generators' amplitude to zero, for example only the voice.

## What you should see

- Voice alone: on the left channel a clear wave; with CANCEL on, a flat line.
- Instruments: they stay in the difference.

## Why it works

The difference amplifier's output is left minus right. The voice is identical in both channels, so it cancels to zero. The guitar is only in the left channel and the flute only in the right, so the difference still contains both (the flute upside down, which the ear does not notice).
