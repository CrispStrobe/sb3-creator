# Expected behaviour

- On start: mono_lcd widget renders, buttons visible, display shows "page0:EV3 Ready".
- Press ▼: page advances to 1 ("page1:Motor Status").
- Press ▼ again: page advances to 2 ("page2:Sensor Data").
- Press ▲: page goes back to 1.
- Press OK: display shows "selected:1".
- Press ▼ from page 2: wraps to page 0.
