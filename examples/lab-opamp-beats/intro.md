# Two op-amp tones in beat

Two op-amp tone generators, almost the same pitch, added together: the sound swells and fades, slow when the pitches are close, faster when they move apart.

**Teaches:** beats, superposition, frequency difference

## What to do

Click **Sim** and listen. Turn DETUNE (potentiometer_15) slowly one way and the other; probe the adder's output with the scope if you like.

## What you should see

- A tone of about 930 Hz that pulses about thirteen times a second.
- Turning DETUNE changes the pulsing speed; where the pitches are equal it stops.

## Why it works

When the two waves are in step they add; half a beat later one is up while the other is down, and they cancel. The faster one gains a whole cycle on the other as often per second as their frequencies differ.
