# Fibre bend on the meter

**Teaches:** light guide, bending loss, emitter follower, meter

## What to do

Click **Sim** and read the meter (analog_meter_8). Then make the loop in the fibre (light_path_9) tighter, step by step, down to a few millimetres.

## What you should see

- About 3.8 V with a gentle loop.
- The tighter the loop, the lower the needle: about 1.6 V at 4 mm.

## Why it works

Light stays inside the fibre because it hits the wall at a shallow angle and is reflected. In a tight bend it hits the wall too steeply and part of it escapes. Less light at the end means less current through the phototransistor, and the transistor after it passes that on to the meter.
