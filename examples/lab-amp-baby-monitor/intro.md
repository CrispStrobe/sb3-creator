# Baby monitor that howls when held too close

A loudspeaker can listen as well as speak. Amplify what it hears in the nursery and play it on an earphone next door — but bring the earphone back to the speaker and the system screams.

**Teaches:** speaker as microphone, multistage amplifier, acoustic feedback

## What to do

Click **Sim** and listen. In the instruments column move the loudspeaker's **distance** slider (how far the earphone is) from 100 down to 3 cm, then back.

## What you should see

- Far away: the earphone plays the voice.
- Close: a loud howl that does not need any sound to start.

## Why it works

Sound moves the speaker's cone and makes a few microvolts in its coil; three amplifier stages make volts of that. The earphone's sound reaches the speaker again: if it arrives stronger than the original, each round trip makes it stronger still, and the amplifier ends up oscillating at full power.
