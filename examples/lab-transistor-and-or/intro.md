# AND and OR from transistors

**Teaches:** logic gates, NAND, NOR, inverter

## What to do

Click **Sim**. Press button A (button_3), then B (button_4), then both together. Watch the green and the yellow LED.

## What you should see

- Green (AND) lights only when you press both buttons.
- Yellow (OR) lights when you press either button, or both.

## Why it works

Two transistors one above the other conduct only if both are switched on: their output goes low only when A and B are pressed, a NAND. Two side by side conduct if either one is on: a NOR. A PNP transistor after each gate turns low into "LED on": NAND becomes AND, NOR becomes OR.
