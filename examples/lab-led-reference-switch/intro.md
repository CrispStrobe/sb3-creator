# An LED as a reference voltage

**Teaches:** transistor switch, reference voltage, threshold

## What to do

Click **Sim** and turn the knob (potentiometer_3) slowly up from the bottom. Note where the green LED comes on. The red LED is lit all the time.

## What you should see

- The green LED stays dark for the first quarter of the travel.
- Just past a quarter it comes on, and soon it is fully bright.

## Why it works

The transistor conducts only when its base is about 0.6 V above its emitter. Here the emitter does not sit at ground but on the red LED, which holds it at about 1.7 V. So the base needs about 2.4 V, a bit more than a quarter of the 9 V across the knob.
