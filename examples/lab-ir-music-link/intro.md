# Music on an infrared beam

Not just on and off: the brightness of an infrared LED can follow music smoothly, and a phototransistor across the room turns the brightness changes back into sound.

**Teaches:** analog modulation, LED brightness, optical receiver

## What to do

Click **Sim** and listen at the earphone. Halve the **function generator**'s amplitude, then change its frequency. Move the receiver with the light path's **knob**.

## What you should see

- The earphone plays the generator's tone.
- Half the amplitude at the generator, half at the earphone.

## Why it works

The amplifier keeps its output at half the supply and adds the music on top, so the LED always carries some current and the music makes it brighter and dimmer. The phototransistor's current follows the light, and the receiver amplifies those small changes.
