# Warm transistor turns the light on

**Teaches:** transistor, temperature, bias

## What to do

Click **Sim**. The LED is dark. Move the transistor's **Temperature** slider (npn_3) to hot air, as if you pointed a hair dryer at it, and wait a few seconds. Then move it back to room. Turn the SET trimmer (potentiometer_6) to shift the switching point.

## What you should see

- Room temperature: the LED is dark.
- Hot air: after a second or two the LED comes on.
- Back at room temperature it goes out again.

## Why it works

The base is held just below the voltage at which the transistor starts to conduct. A warm silicon junction needs about 2 mV less per degree, so the same base voltage drives far more current through a warm transistor: about twenty times more at 70 °C than at room temperature.
