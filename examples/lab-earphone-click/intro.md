## What you see
A battery, a key, a red LED and a magnetic earphone in one loop. Two slide switches reverse the earphone's two wires.

## Try this
1. Click **Sim** and press the key: the LED lights and the earphone gives one click.
2. Keep holding it: silence, although current keeps flowing (the LED stays lit).
3. Let go: a second click.
4. Flip both slide switches, which turns the earphone round, and repeat: the same two clicks.

## What is going on
A magnetic earphone moves its thin iron disc only while the current through its coil changes. Switching on is a change, switching off is a change, a steady current is none, so you hear a click at each end and nothing in between. Which way the current flows does not matter for a click: the disc is pulled either way. To hear a tone instead, the current has to change hundreds or thousands of times a second, which is what the transistor oscillators in the other benches do.
