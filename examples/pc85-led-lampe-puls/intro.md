## What you see
A single white LED breathes gently: slowly brighter, slowly dimmer, endlessly. An LM358 op-amp generates the triangle wave that controls the brightness.

## Try this
1. Click **Sim** — the LED pulses smoothly.
2. The breathing rate depends on R (100 kΩ) and C (1 µF).
3. No digital signal, no steps — brightness changes analogically.
