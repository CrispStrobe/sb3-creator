# Expected behavior

1. Press **GO**. With all four switches off, the four output LEDs stay dark.
2. Enable **PB0** and **PB2**. **PA0** and **PA2** light. **PA1** and **PA3** stay dark.
3. Press **ST**. The four LED outputs hold their last values while the switches remain interactive.
4. Enable **SST** and press **GO** to advance one NMOS 6502 instruction at a time.
