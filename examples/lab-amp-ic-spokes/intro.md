# Spoke rattle: from ticks to a buzz

A card clipped to a bicycle fork rattles in the spokes: tick, tick, tick when you ride slowly, a buzz when you ride fast. This LM386 oscillator makes the same sound. One knob sets the rate, from a few ticks a second to a buzz.

**Teaches:** relaxation oscillator, a variable resistor sets the rate, clicks fusing into a tone

## What to do

Click **Sim** and listen. Turn the rate knob slowly up to the end.

## What you should see

- At the start: about 2.5 ticks a second, the LED blinking with them.
- Turning up: faster and faster ticks, until at full turn they merge into a buzz of about 60 Hz.

## Why it works

The capacitor's push on the amplifier's input fades through the knob's resistance. Less resistance, faster fade, sooner the next flip. Your ear hears separate clicks only up to roughly 15 to 20 a second; faster than that they become a low tone.
