# Two coils, no wire: a pulse through the air

Two windings sit on one core. Each one is an ordinary conductor, yet there is no connection between them. Dump a charged capacitor into one winding and an LED on the other side flashes: the energy crossed over as a changing magnetic field.

**Teaches:** electromagnetic induction, galvanic isolation, why only a change induces anything

## What to do

Click **Sim**. First use the tester: with the slide switch on the left the green LED lights through winding A. Move it right: the LED goes out, because winding B is not connected to winding A. Then press **DUMP** briefly and watch the red LED. Hold DUMP down for a while and see whether anything else happens.

## What you should see

- The tester lights through a winding but not from one winding to the other.
- One short red flash for each press of DUMP, fading over two or three seconds.
- Nothing while you just hold the button.

## Why it works

Current in winding A builds a magnetic field in the core, and winding B sits in that same field. A voltage appears in winding B only while the field changes, which is why the sudden capacitor discharge makes a flash and the steady current afterwards makes none. Transformers, wireless chargers and the tuning coils in a radio all use this.
